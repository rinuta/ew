OUR ICONS

Adriana Lima

Adriana Lima was born on June 12, 1981, in Salvador, Brazil.

She became one of the most iconic Victoria's Secret Angels. She first walked in the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in 1999.

Her career made her one of the most recognizable models in the fashion world.

Tyra Banks

Tyra Banks was born on December 4, 1973, in California, USA.

She became a famous model and television personality. Her first Victoria's Secret Fashion Show was in 1996.

Candice Swanepoel

Candice Swanepoel is a South African fashion model.

She became internationally famous through her work with Victoria's Secret and its fashion shows.
